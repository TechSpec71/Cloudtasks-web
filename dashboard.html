About Us: Connecting Global Ambition with skilled $ Talented freelancers

Our Mission

At Cloudtasks, we believe that quality work shouldn't be limited by borders. Our mission is to bridge the gap between forward-thinking companies and a curated community of skilled professionals. We simplify the outsourcing process by ensuring that every task, no matter how small, is handled with precision and professional integrity.

What We Do

We act as the essential intermediary in the remote work ecosystem.

  • For Companies: We provide a streamlined gateway to outsource complex or high-volume tasks without the overhead of traditional hiring.
  • For Service Providers: We offer a platform where skill meets opportunity, allowing vetted professionals to access meaningful work from global clients.

The Cloudtasks Standard: Vetted & Verified

What sets us apart is our commitment to quality. We don't just "link" people; we curate excellence.

  • Comprehensive Vetting: Every service provider on our platform undergoes an Aligned verification process.
  • Quality Assurance: We Assess output and maintain strict standards, so companies can trust their projects are in safe hands.
  • Secure Logistics:We have Locally Integrated, reliable payment systems in collaboration with local payment merchants to easen the payments and pay out processes and ensure no delays removing friction from remote collaborations.

How Payouts Are Done

Cloudtasks makes payments and payouts as seamless as possible by partnering with selected local merchants to ease the process of handling payments. As different countries have different payment systems preferred by the majority of their citizens, all billings and settlements will be done through selected local merchants to ensure that you receive your payouts in your local currency — based on the country you input in your user profile — and credited to the account or mobile number you provided in your user profile.

Intermediary / Referral Payouts are made within 1–2 business days when the referred persons are approved.

Task Payouts are made within 1–5 business days after we cross-check the submitted task and the client approves your submitted work.

NOTE! To earn from Intermediary / Referral, the person you invite must input your UIA Code under the UIA input when registering and successfully complete their Membership Application, so that the system schedules you for the payout through the local merchant.

CloudTasks Rules

The following are not allowed on our platform:

  • Rule 1 — No False Identity: Use of false identity details is strictly prohibited. If reviewed and found to contain false details, your account will be permanently revoked.
  • Rule 2 — No Proxies or VPNs: No use of proxies or VPNs while undertaking our tasks. Cloudtasks gives every citizen an opportunity to use native accounts, and for credibility of our work, no interference of IPs will be tolerated.
  • Rule 3 — No Scripting and Automation: Using autoclick/fill extensions is strictly forbiden on Clients' projects .
  • Rule 4 — No Disclosure of Client Projects: Do not disclose clients' projects to third parties. Only verified members should handle assigned tasks and projects, unless the task is not marked as classified.
